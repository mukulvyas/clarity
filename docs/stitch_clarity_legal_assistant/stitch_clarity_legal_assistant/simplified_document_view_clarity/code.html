Deductions • Section 9.3

Security Deposit & Move-out Deductions

priority_high ⚠️ Pay Attention — Potential Overcharge

What this means: The landlord attempts to automatically withhold fees for repainting and professional carpet steaming upon move-out, regardless of how cleanly you leave the apartment.

source Original Contract Text (Page 6, Line 110)
“Security Deposit Deductions: Deductions for repainting interior walls and professional steam cleaning will be assessed automatically against the security deposit regardless of unit condition upon surrender.”

Legal Precedent:

Under state tenant protection statutes, landlords cannot deduct for normal, routine wear and tear (such as minor scuffs on walls or expected foot traffic on carpets). Mandatory non-refundable cleaning clauses are routinely struck down in small claims.

Suggested counter-language to email property manager:

“Please amend Section 9.3 to read: 'Deductions for painting and professional carpet cleaning shall be assessed only in the event of damages exceeding normal wear and tear.'”

Early Surrender • Clause 18.2

Early Move-out and Termination Penalties

report_problem High Financial Risk — Double Recovery

What this means: If you must break your lease due to a job relocation or family emergency, the landlord charges a severe double-penalty: a flat $4,200 fine AND you must continue paying rent until they locate another renter.

source Original Contract Text (Page 11, Line 204)
“Clause 18.2: Early Termination Liquidated Damages. Tenant agrees to pay liquidated damages in the amount of two (2) months base rent ($4,200) in addition to all actual rent losses incurred during vacancy until re-rental, forfeiting security deposit.”
balance

Legal Red Flag: “Double Dipping”

Most courts prohibit landlords from both collecting a full liquidated damages penalty AND charging ongoing rent for the vacant months. They cannot double-recover damages while maintaining a duty to mitigate vacancies.

Recommended Action:

Request an standard early-release buyout clause: “Tenant may terminate early upon 60 days advance written notice and payment of a flat fee equal to two months rent, which completely discharges all further obligations.”
