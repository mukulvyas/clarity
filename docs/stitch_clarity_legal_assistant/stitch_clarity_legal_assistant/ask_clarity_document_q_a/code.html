In non-emergencies, no. Your landlord must give you at least 24 hours written notice before entering for routine inspections, maintenance repairs, or prospective tenant walkthroughs.

menu_book Citation: Section 14.1 (Page 7) Click to inspect
“Except in cases of bona fide emergency, Lessor shall provide Lessee not less than 24 hours written notice prior to entry between the hours of 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM.”
