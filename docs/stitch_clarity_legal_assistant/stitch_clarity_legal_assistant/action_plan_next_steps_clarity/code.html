Dear Oakwood Heights Management, Thank you for sending over the lease agreement for Apartment 4B. I am very eager to finalize everything. In reviewing Clause 18.2 regarding early lease termination, I noticed the penalty is set at three full months' rent. Given unforeseen professional transfers, could we agree to an amendment capping this fee at one month's rent along with a 60-day written notice? I am happy to sign the revised agreement promptly upon this adjustment. Thank you for your flexibility! Warm regards, [Your Name]

Stage 02

Questions to Ask the Landlord or a Lawyer

Copy these directly into your messaging thread or bring them up during your lease review call.

Q1

"Can we add an exception to the early termination fee for involuntary job relocation?"

Many institutional landlords include military or career-displacement clauses with 45-day employer proof.

Q2

"Will the landlord provide an itemized move-out checklist for deposit return criteria?"

Prevents unexpected deductions for normal wear and tear by establishing objective cleaning expectations now.

Q3

"Who handles extermination/pest control if issues arise during the first 30 days?"

Clarifies whether the property manager covers immediate remedies or passes costs to the tenant upon occupancy.
