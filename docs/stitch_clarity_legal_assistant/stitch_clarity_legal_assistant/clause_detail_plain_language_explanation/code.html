Clause 18.2: Early Termination Penalty

This clause decides what happens if life circumstances force you to vacate early. As currently written, it places a heavy unilateral burden on you.

Original Contract Wording (Exact Legalese)

“In the event Tenant vacates, abandons, or terminates prior to the Natural Expiration Date, Tenant shall forfeit the full security deposit and remain jointly and severally liable for liquidated damages equal to two (2) months of standard monthly rent, notwithstanding Landlord’s statutory duty to mitigate damages.”

In most jurisdictions, landlords are explicitly required by state law to actively seek a replacement tenant (the “duty to mitigate damages”). They cannot legally double-dip by charging you full damages while collecting rent from a replacement occupant.

This clause attempts to make you waive that statutory safeguard. Many local tenant courts refuse to enforce total deposit forfeitures when bundled with a 2-month termination fee.
